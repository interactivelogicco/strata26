ArtLoop

Creative Community Platform

ArtLoop is a private community platform that helps artists, organizations, and creative groups share works in progress, foster meaningful conversations, and strengthen local creative communities.

Back to projects Follow progress

Built for creative communities.

ArtLoop was designed to give creative communities a calm, focused place to gather online. Instead of chasing algorithms and engagement metrics, it encourages thoughtful discussion, works in progress, events, and lasting relationships between artists and organizations.

Core features

Private Communities

Create invite-only communities where members can safely share ideas and creative work.

Work in Progress

Post artwork, writing, music, photography, or other creative projects for feedback throughout the creative process.

Events & Gatherings

Share exhibitions, workshops, open studios, and community events in one place.

Conversations

Encourage meaningful discussions without the distractions of traditional social media.

Creative Profiles

Give every member a simple profile that highlights their work and community involvement.

Moderation Tools

Provide administrators with thoughtful tools that keep communities welcoming and organized.
